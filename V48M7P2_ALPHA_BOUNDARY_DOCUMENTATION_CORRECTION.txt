V48M7P2 ALPHA-BOUNDARY DOCUMENTATION CORRECTION

The executed V47/V48 production code treats a fitted alpha within 0.01 of
either imposed bound (0.2 or 5.0) as an invalid band fit. The canonical
denominator and all dependent injection products were generated with this
veto. A later revision-documentation generator incorrectly described the flag
as diagnostic only. V48M7P2 corrects that documentation; it does not alter the
scientific arrays or denominator.

Audited counts from v48b_object_band_fits.csv:
- 736 attempted object-band fits
- 717 optimizer-successful fits
- 179 successful lower-boundary fits
- 133 successful upper-boundary fits
- 312 successful fits rejected by either boundary
- 405 valid interior band fits
- 303 objects with at least one valid interior fit
- 65 objects with no valid band fit
- 64 of those 65 have successful fits only at a boundary
- 1 has no optimizer-successful band fit

The authoritative executable definition remains in
scripts/59_run_v48b_canonical_baseline_scatter.py.
